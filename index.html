FAQ

The questions you'd ask anyway.

Is this another personality test?

No. CHECKMATCH is built on observed behavior — daily check-ins, verified activities, wearable signals — over a six-week loop. Personality tests describe; we measure. Your archetype sharpens or shifts as you actually live.

How is CompScore calculated?

It's a weighted read across four dimensions — Drive, Recovery, Conflict, Trust — calibrated by your archetype pair and updated weekly from new inputs. You see the math, not just the number.

Why animal companions?

Because the way you live with the non-humans in your house is some of the highest-fidelity behavioral data there is. Built with veterinary behaviorists, equine trainers, and applied ethologists.

What does "verified" actually mean?

When you log an activity (e.g., "walk, no agenda, 25 min"), we cross-check against your wearable's heart rate + movement signal and your calendar's open-window reading. Self-report alone doesn't move your score.

Who sees my data?

You. Plus anyone you explicitly add to your Live Circle for shared reads. Never sold, never used to train external models. Full export at any time, in PDF and JSON.

How long until I get a real read?

The taste test gives you a directional read in 90 seconds. Your first stable archetype lands at the end of week 2. The first compatibility report at week 4.
